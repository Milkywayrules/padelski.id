## answer

The recreational padel app market splits into **three layers**: (1) **global club-booking marketplaces** led by Playtomic and MATCHi; (2) **Indonesia-native padel platforms** led by Courtside and the multi-sport super-app AYO, with Padler as a match/community challenger; (3) **regional SEA club apps** (Pop Padel, Prime Padel, Love All) plus **UK/EU white-label operators** (Nettla, Padel Mates) that clubs adopt rather than players choosing directly.

For **recreational players** (not federation-official ranking), the decisive battleground in Indonesia is **Courtside vs AYO vs Padler**, with Playtomic present mainly on **Bali club integrations** rather than nationwide Java dominance [2][10]. Globally, Playtomic sets the **de facto recreational skill standard** (0–7 leveling, cross-club) [1][12], while MATCHi optimizes **multi-sport club operations** with lighter algorithmic scoring [5][14].

**padelski.id whitespace (inference, not verified):** None of the verified competitors combine **web-first PlaySession grouping**, **live unofficial scoring**, and **SlotClaim waitlist mechanics** as a single recreational layer on top of booking. Closest overlaps: Courtside (open play + score history) [2], Padler (hosted sessions + stats feed) [4], Playtomic (open matches + ELO) [1].

## reasoning

1. **T2 Hunter (discovery)** — Mapped 12 consumer-relevant platforms across global marketplaces, ID natives, SEA club apps, and UK white-label. Excluded WPT/FIP official apps and federation ranking systems per scope (recreational/unofficial).

2. **T2 Practitioner (deep read)** — Extracted vendor-primary claims from Playtomic [1], Courtside [2], AYO [3], Padler [4], MATCHi [5], Padel Mates [6], Nettla [7], RacketPal [8], Pop Padel [9][11], and Playtomic Indonesia club page [10]. Secondary blogs [12][14][15] used only for scoring methodology where primary docs blocked.

3. **T2 Skeptic (challenge)** — Vendor scale metrics (Courtside 300k, AYO 900k) are **self-reported**, not audited. Playtomic Indonesia presence is **clustered (Bali)**, not proof of Java dominance [10]. Padel Mates ELO 0–7 is **T2-only** [15]. Prime Padel, Love All, TiePlayer lack verified primary fetch in this run.

4. **T2 Arbiter (conflicts)** — No material factual contradictions on verified rows. Playtomic vs MATCHi level systems differ by design (algorithmic vs self-declared) [12][14]; treated as complementary models, not conflict.

5. **Ranking method** — Ordinal ranks weight **player-side recreational utility** per column definition. Geo table double-weights Indonesia/SEA as requested. Ranks are **structured analyst estimates**, not survey-based share.

6. **padelski.id mapping (inference)** — Product primitives (PlaySession, live scoring, SlotClaim, unofficial community) address gaps where incumbents either (a) optimize club SaaS, (b) treat scoring as post-match history, or (c) bind skill to marketplace ELO rather than friend-group sessions.
